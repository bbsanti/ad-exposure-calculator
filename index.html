    How to measure ad exposure

    Exposure here means share of screen: how much of the picture an ad covers in a single frame. Take a screenshot of the moment, mark each ad, and read the share from the panel on the right.

    1. Get a screenshot

      • From a recording: pause on the frame and export a still at full size. In Premiere Pro, use Export Frame (the camera button under the Program monitor). In VLC, use Video › Take Snapshot, which saves to your Pictures folder. Either way the still keeps the broadcast size, such as 1920 × 1080.
      • From a live stream or web player: press Win+Shift+S on Windows or Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 on a Mac, select the picture, then paste it here with Ctrl+V. If the capture includes player controls or the page around the video, use Frame (F) to drag around just the picture so the rest isn't counted.
      • Load it: click Open screenshots, drag files onto the page, or paste. Load several to measure a whole set in one go.

    2. Mark each ad

      • Under By sponsor, add the sponsor's name or click an existing one. New shapes go to the highlighted sponsor.
      • Box (B): drag around a flat, square-on ad such as an LED board or an on-screen graphic.
      • Polygon (P): click around an angled ad such as a floor logo, then press Enter or click the first point to close it.
      • Trace only the part you can see. If a player covers part of a board, leave that part out, for example with a box on either side. Where shapes overlap, the shared area is counted once.
      • To adjust a shape, drag it or its handles, nudge it with the arrow keys, or type exact numbers under Selected shape.

    3. Read the exposure

      • Total ad coverage, top right: the share of the frame that all ads cover together. This is the screenshot's exposure figure.
      • By sponsor › Share: each sponsor's exposure in this screenshot. Pixels is the same area as a pixel count.
      • The tag on each shape shows that shape's share, and each thumbnail shows its screenshot's total.
      • Copy table pastes into Sheets or Excel with one row per sponsor per screenshot plus an All ads row. Download CSV saves the same table, and Annotated PNG saves the screenshot with the shapes and percentages drawn on it.
      • Percentages don't depend on the screenshot's size. If you need pixel counts at broadcast size, set Count pixels at to 1920 × 1080.

      Share of screen = ad pixels ÷ frame pixels × 100
